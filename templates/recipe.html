Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

A creamy and comforting chicken pasta made with garlic, herbs, and Parmesan cheese.

35 minutes 4 servings

Ingredients

Ingredients currently available in your inventory are shown normally. Missing ingredients are faded.

Chicken Breast 500 g
Penne Pasta 300 g
Garlic 4 cloves
Heavy Cream 200 ml
Parmesan Cheese 75 g
Olive Oil 2 tbsp
Italian Herbs 1 tsp
Salt & Pepper To taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.

2

Prepare the chicken

Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs.

3

Cook the chicken

Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden brown and fully cooked.

4

Prepare the sauce

Add minced garlic and cook for one minute. Add the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese. Stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.

5

Combine the ingredients

Add the cooked pasta and chicken to the sauce. Toss everything together until evenly coated.

6

Serve

Divide the pasta between four plates. Garnish with Parmesan cheese and fresh herbs. Serve immediately.
