Features

Expiration Reminders

Receive reminders before your food expires and reduce unnecessary waste.

Smart Shopping List

Nutrition Tracker

About Us

Food Logistics is a mini project developed as part of the 2nd Year academic project for SOE, Ajeenkya DY Patil University.

Our project focuses on reducing household food waste through food inventory management, recipe generation, expiration reminders, smart shopping lists, and nutrition tracking.
